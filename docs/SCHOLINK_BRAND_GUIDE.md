# Scholink Brand Guide

**Status:** Working brand direction  
**Product:** Scholink  
**Tagline:** Connecting Schools. Simplifying Operations.

## Brand position

Scholink is a school operations platform for managing student identity and the daily workflows built around it. It is not positioned as only an ID-card scanner or a generic school ERP.

The brand should communicate:
- **Trust:** student records and access decisions must be handled responsibly.
- **Clarity:** staff should understand what to do with minimal training.
- **Reliability:** core school workflows must remain usable during internet outages.
- **Connection:** identity, gate activity, meals, attendance and reporting belong to one coherent system.

## Visual direction

| Scholink Teal | #0F9D91 | Primary actions, active states, positive progress |
| Deep Teal | #08766E | Hover/pressed states and strong accents |

| Surface | #FFFFFF | Cards, dialogs and panels |
| Text | #172B4D | Primary text |
| Muted Text | #64748B | Secondary labels and metadata |
| Border | #E2E8F0 | Dividers and field outlines |
| Warning | #B7791F | Warnings requiring attention |
| Danger | #B42318 | Errors, revoked credentials and destructive actions |

Use colour semantically and consistently. Do not rely on colour alone to communicate status; pair it with text and, where useful, an icon.

### Typography

Use **Inter** or a metrically similar sans-serif. Establish a clear hierarchy:
- Page title: 24–28sp mobile / 28–32px web
- Section title: 18–20sp / 20–22px
- Body: 14–16sp / 14–16px
- Supporting metadata: 12–13sp / 12–13px

Prioritise readable text, adequate contrast and comfortable touch targets. Do not shrink text to fit dense dashboards.

### Logo concept

Use a simple, ownable symbol built from two interlocking or connected geometric forms that subtly suggest an **S**. Pair it with a lowercase or title-case **Scholink** wordmark. The mark must remain legible in monochrome, at small app-icon sizes and on printed ID cards.

This is a design brief, not a final trademark-cleared logo. Avoid copying an existing education or software brand's mark.

## Interface principles

1. **Task-first layouts:** make the next action obvious for each staff role.
2. **Operational clarity:** show sync state, last successful sync and actionable failures.
3. **Low-friction scanning:** keep the scanner prominent for Gate and Meals staff.
4. **Consistent components:** use the same spacing, fields, buttons, status chips and navigation patterns across roles.
5. **Useful empty states:** explain what is missing and how an authorised user can resolve it.
6. **Accessible states:** provide loading, success, warning, error and offline states with text labels.
7. **Responsive web:** support desktop and tablet first, with usable narrow layouts.
8. **No decorative complexity:** avoid excessive gradients, glass effects, animation and oversized metric cards.

## Product naming

- **Scholink Dashboard:** browser-based school administration.
- **Scholink Staff:** Android application for school staff.
- **Scholink Identity:** student profiles, identity records and QR credentials.
- **Scholink Gate:** gate verification and entry/exit records.
- **Scholink Meals:** meal eligibility and serving records.
- **Scholink Attendance:** attendance capture and reporting.
- **Scholink Insights:** operational reports and summaries.

Module names describe product capabilities; they do not imply every module is already implemented.

## Brand safeguards

Before public launch:
- Check company-name, trademark, domain and app-store availability in relevant markets.
- Commission or create an original logo and verify its similarity to existing marks.
- Confirm colour contrast and usability on actual low-cost Android devices.
- Keep LTC branding confined to the first school deployment; do not bake it into the commercial identity.
